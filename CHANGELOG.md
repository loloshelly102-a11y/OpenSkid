# OpenSkid changelog

What changed for the user. No internals, no process notes.

## Combat

- KillAura: GrimAC, Watchdog, Polar, Intave autoblocks and rotations, KeepRange, SmoothAim, gapple pause, humanizer (miss chance, target-switch pause), Threat target sort.
- Velocity: 18 modes including Intave, GrimAC, Matrix, Polar, Legit, Tick, Zip, Karhu, MatrixReverse, MatrixFull, OldGrim.
- Criticals: 15 modes including Hypixel, Lag, Matrix, Timer.
- AutoClicker: LowCPS, Record, Extra and Extra+ humanized tiers, double-click emulation, fatigue scheduler.
- BlockHit: Manual, Legit, Combo, FakeBlock modes. SprintReset and MoreKB WTap. HitSelect presets.
- New: AutoBlock, AutoWeapon, ArmorBreaker (axe swap to shred armor), JumpReset, BurstClicker (drag-style click bursts), ProjectileAimBot, ThrowAura Aimed mode, KeepRange keeper, LegitReach.
- BackTrack: new BUFFER mode. LagRange: real-position box plus Repel mode (holds packets in range, dumps the burst on distance, timeout, or your own hit).
- FakeLag PULSE mode, Blink HOLD mode, Displace scan sliders, SmartBlinker auto-blink.

## Movement

- Fly: 11 modes. Speed: Polar, Vulcan, GrimAC, Intave, BlocksMC. LongJump: GrimBoat, GrimVelocity, Hypixel, Fireball, Vulcan.
- NoSlow: GrimAC, Hypixel, Intave, OldGrim, Vanilla. NoFall: Legit, Vulcan, Matrix. AntiVoid: 7 modes. Jesus: 4 modes.
- New: BridgeAssist (plus Telly mode), Clutch with hold-to-arm key, WaterClutch, LadderClutch, Phase, Step, Spider wall climb, Freecam scouting, Parkour, NoWeb escape modes.
- Sprint: Legit, Omni, bypass. Timer hop and balance modes. Tower: 18 modes plus auto-tunneling.

## World and building

- Scaffold: BlockIn surround option, place jitter timing, slave-to-Tower arbitration.
- BedNuker: Instant break, cover-first, spawn anchor, KillAura yield. AutoBedDef tiers with swap-back. New BedDefender.
- New: Nuker, AutoPlace, LegitScaffold, BlockLadder, TunnelEngine auto-tunneling.

## Disabler and safety

- Disabler preset selector: Polar, Intave, GrimExtreme, GrimSpec, Mineland, Hypixel, HypixelMotion, KKCraft, BlocksMC, plus Custom.
- New: ModSpoofer, ExploitFixer, AntiFalseFlag, GhostBlock, PingSpoof.
- CheatDetector (8 per-player checks), StaffDetector (tablist, chat, vanish watch, auto-leave), MurderDetector, BedProximityAlert, PartyDetector.

## Visuals and HUD

- TargetHUD: new styles, elastic and scale animations, color modes. TargetESP: Jello, Skid, Nova, Glow outline styles. ESP Glow style.
- New overlays: BedPlates, FKCounter, ClosestPlayerHUD, LeapModeHUD, MegaWallsDetector, FireBallPredict, DamageTags, TNTTimer, PotionHUD, Indicators approaching filter, FallIndicator, InventoryHUD, KeyStrokes.
- New: KillEffect (lightning, sound, particles), EntityCulling (hides unseen entities for FPS).
- Animations: expanded swing animation list. ViewClip camera distance and no-clip options.
- Capes: 49 bundled capes plus custom PNG support. Watermark styles including image mode.

## Sounds

- Startup jingle on the main menu. Toggle sounds with Skid, Ghost, Macro sets and volume. KillSounds with 7 selectable sounds and volume. Notifications module with sound settings.

## Minigames

- BedWars: alerts, BedTracker, BridgeInfo, FireBallPredict, BedProximityAlert. SkyWars: alerts module. Duels: stats. Pit: utils. WoolWars, MurderMystery, SumoFences, SpeedBuilders modules.
- AutoPlay, AutoGG, AutoReconnect, AutoRequeue, AutoWho, AutoBuy, KillSults, KillMessage, PlayerList, ViewPackets, Spammer, Panic.

## ClickGUI

- 6 styles: Normal, Legacy, Legacy+, Nova, Modern, Cards. Hover tooltip on every module. Search, themes, frame options. Every module listed in every style.

## Accounts, scripts, configs

- AltManager: cracked and Microsoft login, ban tracker, alt screen.
- Script runtime for local files. Shareable online configs with applied-versus-failed counts.
- AutoRespawn on death. PearlSaver void rescue. AutoRod with aim, AutoArmor, AutoPot, AutoSoup, Refill, ChestStealer and InvManager loot humanizers.
